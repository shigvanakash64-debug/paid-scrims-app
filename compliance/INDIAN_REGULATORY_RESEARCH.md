## Esports and online money games
The statutory framework distinguishes esports and online money games. Paid-entry and prize mechanics, stakes, wallet redemption rights and prize sources determine factual characterization. Do not infer classification from a skill-based label or the presence of a game name.

## DPDP Act and Rules
- Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and 2025 Rules are on a phased commencement schedule.
- One-year provisions have reached their stated commencement date; later operational provisions remain pending until the applicable 18-month date.
- No conclusion should describe all obligations as either wholly effective or wholly future.

## IT Rules
- The IT (Intermediary Guidelines and Digital Media Ethics Code) Rules, 2021 include grievance and due-diligence provisions.
- Whether Clutch Zone is an intermediary or a significant intermediary depends on actual service functions.
- The need for a specific grievance officer is **UNRESOLVED** until counsel confirms platform classification and applicable rules.

## Consumer protection
The Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and e-commerce rules may apply to the platform's customer-facing role. Refund, grievance and deceptive-promise practices require particular review.
