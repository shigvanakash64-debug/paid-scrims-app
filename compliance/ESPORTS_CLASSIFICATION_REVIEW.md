# Esports Classification Review

**Status:** UNRESOLVED; formal classification verification required.

## Configured formats
The database supports 33 named games and several formats including direct modes, custom tournaments, per-kill formats and team-vs-team formats.

## Required determination
For each materially different paid format, determine:
- Whether entry money is consideration, stake or prize-linked payment.
- Whether users expect monetary winnings.
- Who supplies the prize pool.
- Whether bonuses or deposits are used.
- Whether participants have a legal right to withdraw credits.
- Whether the tournament is an esports competition under applicable law.
- Whether the specific game is officially recognized for the applicable format.

## Current policy position
The website must describe paid participation only as subject to applicable law and relevant classification. It must not say that all 33 games are esports or that paid entries are legally approved/exempt.
